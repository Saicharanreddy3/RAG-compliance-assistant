# Access Control Standard

## 1. Overview

This standard defines requirements for granting, reviewing, and revoking access to information
systems. It supports the Information Security Policy and maps to control families AC-2, AC-3,
and AC-6.

## 2. Account Management (AC-2)

Access is granted on the principle of least privilege. Every account must be traceable to a named
individual; shared accounts are prohibited except for documented break-glass accounts, which must
have credentials stored in a sealed vault and rotated after every use.

Access requests must be approved by the requester's line manager and the system owner. Approval
records must be retained for the life of the account plus two (2) years.

## 3. Access Reviews

System owners must perform a documented access review at least quarterly for systems classified as
Critical, and at least annually for all other systems. Reviews must confirm that each account is
still required and that its privilege level remains appropriate. Findings must be remediated within
thirty (30) days of the review.

## 4. Privileged Access (AC-6)

Privileged access must be time-bound and must not exceed eight (8) hours per elevation request.
All privileged sessions must be recorded, and the recordings retained in line with the Data
Retention and Disposal Policy. Standing administrative access is prohibited on production systems.

## 5. Revocation

Access must be revoked within twenty-four (24) hours of a termination or role change. For
involuntary terminations, access must be revoked before the employee is notified, and no later
than the effective termination time.

## 6. Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access, all administrative interfaces, and
any system processing Personal Data. Passwords must be at least fourteen (14) characters. Password
rotation on a fixed schedule is not required where multi-factor authentication is enforced and
credential compromise monitoring is in place.

## 7. Exceptions and Enforcement

Non-compliance identified during an access review is tracked as a finding with an assigned owner
and a remediation due date. Findings open past their due date are escalated to the Chief
Information Security Officer monthly.
