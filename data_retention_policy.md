# Data Retention and Disposal Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes minimum retention periods and disposal requirements for records
created or received by the organization. It applies to all employees, contractors, and
third-party processors handling company or customer data, in any format, in any jurisdiction
in which the organization operates.

## 2. Definitions

"Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
"Record" means information created, received, and maintained as evidence of a business activity,
regardless of medium. "Disposal" means the permanent, irreversible destruction of a record such
that it cannot be reconstructed by any reasonable means.

## 3. Retention Periods

### 3.1 Financial Records

Financial records, including general ledgers, invoices, and tax filings, must be retained for a
minimum of seven (7) years from the end of the fiscal year to which they relate. Records subject
to an open audit must be retained until the audit concludes and any resulting dispute is resolved,
even where that exceeds seven years.

### 3.2 Audit Logs

System audit logs, including authentication events, privileged access events, and configuration
changes, must be retained for a minimum of eighteen (18) months. At least ninety (90) days of
audit log data must remain immediately searchable in hot storage; the remainder may be moved to
archival storage provided it can be restored within seventy-two (72) hours of a documented request.

### 3.3 Customer Personal Data

Customer Personal Data must be deleted within thirty (30) days of account closure, except where a
longer period is required by law or by an active legal hold. Where a legal hold applies, the data
custodian must record the basis for the hold and review it every ninety (90) days.

### 3.4 Employment Records

Employment records must be retained for the duration of employment plus six (6) years following
the termination date. Records relating to workplace injury must be retained for thirty (30) years.

## 4. Legal Holds

Upon receipt of a litigation hold notice from the Legal department, all disposal activity for the
affected records must cease immediately. Automated deletion jobs covering the affected data set
must be suspended within twenty-four (24) hours of the notice. Failure to suspend automated
deletion is a reportable control failure.

## 5. Disposal Requirements

Electronic media must be sanitized using a method that renders data unrecoverable, and the
custodian must produce a certificate of destruction retained for three (3) years. Paper records
containing Personal Data must be cross-cut shredded. Disposal performed by a third party requires
a written contract that includes the right to audit the vendor's disposal process.

## 6. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from both the Data Protection Officer and the
Chief Information Security Officer. Approved exceptions expire after twelve (12) months and must
be re-approved to remain in effect.
